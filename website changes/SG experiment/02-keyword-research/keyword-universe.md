## The framing you gave (and the data confirms)

People search in three modes, almost never the fourth:
1. **Pain / symptom** — "why isn't this working," "cheap X" (they feel a problem)
2. **Outcome** — "get more X" (they want a result)
3. **Service** — "X agency singapore," "X pricing" (they're ready to hire)
4. **Technology** — "ai chatbot," "automation" (rarely; only the technical or the curious)

**What the SG data proves:** buyers overwhelmingly search **SERVICE** terms and **price-PAIN** terms. They barely search **OUTCOME** phrasing, and **TECHNOLOGY** terms are either high-volume-wrong-intent (consumers wanting a free chatbot) or tiny. So: **build SERVICE pages, seed PAIN/OUTCOME as blog + AEO content, never lead with TECHNOLOGY.**

## BUCKET 2 — PAIN / SYMPTOM. Two layers: price-pain (searched) and problem-pain (spoken).

**Price-pain (shows in search, low KD, buyer-adjacent):**
| keyword | vol | KD | CPC |
|---|---|---|---|
| affordable web design singapore | 170 | **0** | $13.6 |
| cheap web design (in) singapore | 170 | 3–33 | $13.6 |
| affordable / cheap website design singapore | 170 | 7–13 | $13.6 |

("affordable / cheap + service + singapore" is a reliable SG pattern — replicate it: *affordable lead generation singapore*, *affordable seo singapore*, *cheap chatbot singapore*. Low competition, price-sensitive SME intent.)

**Problem-pain (does NOT show in keyword tools — lives in forums/DMs/LinkedIn; harvested via social recon). Use as blog + AEO content, not landing pages:**
- "cold outreach not working in singapore" / "low reply rate cold email singapore" (documented: SG averages 1–3 leads/week, <2% reply)
- "leads not converting" / "prospects go silent" / "clients ghosting after quote"
- "spending too much time following up leads" / "manual follow up"
- "missed calls losing customers" / "no one answers after hours"
- "no-shows for appointments" / "clients don't turn up"
- "not enough leads for my business" / "how to get clients in singapore"
- "is cold calling still legal singapore" (DNC/PDPA anxiety — a real SG-specific pain)
- "website gets traffic but no enquiries" (conversion pain → unsafe.design landing-page angle)

**The single most important SG behavioural insight:** SG buyers are **silent researchers**. They read the cold email, don't reply, then check your website and LinkedIn before ever raising a hand. This is why a low-search market still rewards on-page credibility: SEO is not how they *find* you, it is how they *validate* you after outbound or referral. Every page must survive that silent check.

## BUCKET 3 — OUTCOME. Real in the mind, thin in SG search.

Almost nothing here carries volume: "build landing page" (50), "how to build landing page" (20), "how to get whatsapp business account" (30). Buyers feel outcomes but type services. **Use outcome phrasing for headlines, ad copy, and blog titles, not as SEO page targets:** "get more qualified leads without hiring an SDR," "book more meetings on autopilot," "turn missed calls into booked jobs."
