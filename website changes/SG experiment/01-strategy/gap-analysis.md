# The Gap: What Singapore Searches vs What We Want to Sell

**Date:** 17 August 2026
**Total DataForSEO spend to date:** $0.487 of the $5 ceiling (balance ~$50.04). Cache: `dataforseo-cache/`.
**Method:** mined the actual ranked-keyword bases of 8 SG competitors (awebstar, beyond.agency, closegen, voltade, 41labs, aistudio, callbox, mediaone) + long-tail suggestions + 17 live SG SERPs. This shows what SG *actually* searches, not what we hoped it searches.

---

## The headline

The low-hanging fruit is **not "AI agent" anything. It is lead generation.** Singapore searches the plain, high-intent service terms ("singapore lead generation", "lead generation agency", "b2b marketing agency singapore") at **near-zero keyword difficulty and $20–45 CPC**, and those SERPs are owned by small, beatable SG agencies. That lane is bottom-of-funnel, high-value, low-competition, and it is literally the service we want to sell. It is the fastest realistic path to SG traction. We enter it as an AI-native, forward-deployed lead-gen team and rank on the terms buyers already type.

## Two perspectives, and where they intersect

### What WE wanted to rank for
"ai marketing agent", "ai sdr singapore", "sales automation singapore", "ai lead generation singapore", "ai voice agent singapore". **Search volume in SG: essentially zero.** Prefixing "ai" kills the volume. The buyer is not yet searching for the technology; they search for the outcome.

Four demand clusters, ranked by fit x winnability:

**2. WEB DESIGN + SEO cluster — huge SG demand, now a deliberate upsell lane (updated).**
"web development singapore" and 15 variants at **3,600/mo each, KD 1–35, $25 CPC**; "seo package/pricing singapore" at 320/mo, **KD 0**, pure pricing intent. This is what SG SMEs overwhelmingly buy. It is awebstar's entire business.

Updated decision (Aditya, 17 Aug): do NOT ignore this. Club it as a **retainer upsell** on top of the agent work. The wedge is the AI agent (lead gen / SDR); once a client is in, offer the custom-services layer they actually keep paying for: **high-conversion landing page design + SEO**, delivered through our existing sister agency **unsafe.design** (branding, UI/UX, landing pages). This packages cleanly: agents bring the outcome (leads), the landing page + SEO retainer captures and compounds it. Concretely:
- A `/high-converting-landing-pages-singapore/` (or landing-page + SEO service page) that targets the "landing page design" and "seo package singapore" (KD 0) BoFu terms, cross-linked to unsafe.design as the delivery arm.
- On the lead-gen pages, a "we also build the page that converts the leads" section linking the same.
- Keep it Phase 1.5 (right after the lead-gen beachhead), not the opening move: the agent is still the differentiated hook; landing-page + SEO is the retainer that makes an SG client worth keeping. This also finally gives us a legitimate reason to compete for the high-volume web/SEO SG terms without pretending to be a web-design shop.

**3. CHATBOT cluster — high volume, wrong intent, hard.**
"ai chatbot" 12,100/mo but KD 46–74 and the intent is consumers wanting to *use* a chatbot (plus bank navigational: dbs/uob/posb/singtel chatbot). Not agency-hire. Skip the head terms.

**4. AI AUTOMATION / GRANTS — thin or owned.**
"ai automation" 390/mo KD 17 but the SERP is Salesforce/Workato/Microsoft/AWS (global, informational). "ai grant singapore" 210 but government-owned. Neither is a Phase 1 lane.

## Why my first seed list missed this

I seeded "ai lead generation singapore" (0 vol). The winning term is "singapore lead generation" (KD 0, 110 vol). SG buyers search the **plain outcome term**, not the AI-prefixed one. Competitor keyword-mining surfaced it; a top-down seed list never would. Lesson locked for future rounds: mine competitors first, seed second.
