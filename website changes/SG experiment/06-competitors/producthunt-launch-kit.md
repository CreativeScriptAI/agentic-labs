## Maker's first comment (the single most important asset - post it the second you launch)

Hey Product Hunt 👋

I'm Aditya, founder of Agentic AI Labs. I built AI Visibility Checker because of a shift I kept seeing: buyers no longer Google ten links and choose. They ask ChatGPT or Perplexity "who should I hire for X" and get one answer with three names in it. If you are not one of those names, you are simply not in the running, and most businesses have no idea whether they are.

So this tool does two things, free, in about a minute:

1. It asks the real questions your buyers ask across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI, Gemini, Grok and Claude, and shows whether those engines name you or your competitors (your "share of voice").
2. It audits your site the way an AI reads it, scoring six areas (technical, speed, content, discoverability, experience, authority) and listing the fixes in priority order.

No signup to see your score. The full report and fix list land in your inbox.

Honest note: this is early and the answer-engine check is rolling out engine by engine, so I would love your feedback, especially edge cases where the result looks wrong. What would make this genuinely useful for you? I will be here all day.

(And yes, we ran it on ourselves first.)

## Launch-day plan
- **Timing:** PH day resets 12:01am PT = **3:01pm Singapore time**. Launch right at reset for a full day on the board. Tue-Thu are the strongest days; avoid weekends.
- **Hunter:** self-launch is fine now (hunters matter less than they used to). If you know a PH-active person, let them hunt it; otherwise post it yourself.
- **First hour:** post the maker's comment immediately. Tell your network it is live (email/WhatsApp/LinkedIn) and ask them to *check it out* - **do NOT ask for upvotes or offer anything for votes; that breaks PH rules and can get you delisted.** Just share the link.
- **All day:** reply to every single comment within minutes. Engagement (comments) is what PH's ranking rewards, more than raw votes.
- **Assets to have open:** this doc, the gallery folder, the maker comment.
- **After:** the backlink and the PH page persist regardless of rank. Add the "Featured on Product Hunt" badge to the site footer or the checker page (extra internal signal + social proof).

## Honest expectations
- The backlink + referral spike are the guaranteed wins, and they directly fix the "zero referral traffic" the audit flagged.
- Ranking top-5 needs real engagement; a quiet launch still gets you the link and some traffic, just less. Either way it is worth doing.
- One launch will not move position 27 alone. It is link #1 of the 5-10 this quarter (pair with the tool/AI directories: OpenAlternative, DevHunt, There's An AI For That, Futurepedia, and brand-mention reclamation).
