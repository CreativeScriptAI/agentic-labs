## 2. WHAT WE CAN LEARN (the strategic lessons)

1. **Tool-led lead capture beats brochure pages.** Both competitors put an interactive tool above the fold as the primary CTA. This validates our entire wedge thesis (free AI-Visibility tool = the lead magnet). Lesson: the tool is not a "nice to have" in Phase A, it is the front door. Ship it before more service pages.
2. **Local + vertical long-tail is an uncontested land-grab in SG.** Neighborhood x industry x service = hundreds of low-KD pages. Our Bucket C is almost all KD 0. The competitors confirm the terrain is open; whoever publishes the honest, useful version first wins the citations.
3. **Transparent pricing is a trust wedge.** Comp 1 shows a number; Comp 2 hides it. In a market full of "contact us for quote," a real SGD range earns trust and pre-qualifies. We should be the transparent one on the money pages.
4. **Comedic / opinionated copy stands out** in a category of identical corporate voices, and opinionated content is more likely to be quoted by LLMs. Worth deploying on blog/AEO spokes.
5. **Package services with "ideal for" segments.** Comp 2's "Ideal for: SG Clinics, Law Firms, F&B..." makes a generic service feel bespoke and improves long-tail match. Cheap to add to our cards.
6. **AEO / GEO is now table stakes in the pitch, and it is the one claim they cannot back.** Comp 2 flags "AI SEO Agency Singapore — Gemini & ChatGPT Ready" and "AI Search (GEO & SGE) Ready" in its ticker. They *say* it. We can *prove* it (our name comes up when you ask ChatGPT / Perplexity about getting more customers — the dogfood proof). This is the single biggest gift they handed us: they have surfaced the demand and staked a claim they have no evidence for.

**The meta-lesson:** both sites are all presentation, no proof. Polished template + fabricated stats + placeholder client counts. In a market this credulous about design, **verifiable proof is the scarce asset.** Our whole play is to be the one with receipts.
