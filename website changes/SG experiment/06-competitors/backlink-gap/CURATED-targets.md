# Backlink gap: curated targets (3 Sept 2026)

Source: DataForSEO referring_domains for tryagentikai.com + 5 SG SEO competitors (oom.com.sg 1399 ref domains, firstpagedigital.sg 1126, mediaone.co 166; awebstar.sg + impossiblemarketing.sg returned 0). Our site: 29 referring domains, of which ~28 are spam. Cost: US$0.2231.

Gap = domains linking to 2+ competitors but not to us. 206 raw; the vast majority are SEO/bookmark spam and are ignored. Below is the filtered, worth-pursuing set.

## First: clean up our own profile
Our 29 referring domains are almost all toxic (casino / poker / pharma / URL-shortener spam: ufabet, betwinner, bestnz-poker, quero.party, betulcrime, etc.). Only creativescript.org is a real link. Action: review these in Google Search Console and disavow the obvious spam. We are effectively starting link-building from zero, which is the real gap, not any single domain.

## Tier 1 — real, relevant, pursue first
Editorially real sites, Singapore / SEA or AI-niche relevant. A genuine mention or listing here is worth chasing.
- topdevs.ai — AI developer/agency directory, directly on-niche. Get listed.
- techbehemoths.com — global B2B agency directory (IT/dev/marketing). Free agency profile.
- builtinsingapore.com — SG startup/tech community. Company profile / contributed content.
- expatliving.sg — established SG lifestyle magazine. Real editorial.
- expatchoice.asia — SG/expat directory + editorial.
- superadrianme.com — well-known SG lifestyle/tech blogger. Outreach for a feature.
- thailand-business-news.com, vietnamnews.vn, thebruneian.news — SEA business press, useful if we run a regional angle / press release.
- asianspectator.com, asiainsiders.net — SEA news aggregators.

## Tier 2 — Singapore "best of" directories (easy citations)
Listicle/directory sites; often a free or low-cost submission. Low individual value but easy, relevant, local NAP citations.
- bestinsingapore.co, bestofsingapore.co, topinsingapore.sg, threebestrated.sg, finestservices.com.sg, webbo.sg, bestwebdesign.sg, callbox.com.sg

## Tier 3 — pay-to-publish PR farms (real DA, low editorial trust)
Sponsored guest-post sites. Google discounts these and they can look manipulative in volume. Only worth it if cheap and used sparingly, never as the core strategy.
- techbullion.com, ventsmagazine.com, theambiencenews.com, strategydriven.com, smallbiztechnology.com, techicy.com, techkow.co, spottedcow.media, valasys.com

## Avoid (spam / competitor-owned)
SEO/bookmark directories and competitor properties in the raw list: seosingapore.co, bestseo.sg, digitalmarket.sg, bestseosingapore.com.sg, seoconsultant.com.sg, worldseodirectory.com, w3bdirectory.com, video-bookmark.com, vuink.com, wayranks.com, siteprice.org, shortenurls.eu, xivermectin.com, and similar. No value; several are toxic.

## Strongest play (given competitors rank without deep links)
oom.com.sg and firstpagedigital.sg have 1000+ referring domains but most are the same low-quality directory/PR farms. We do not need to match their count. A smaller set of genuinely relevant links (Tier 1 + a few Tier 2 local citations) plus the on-page E-E-A-T and citation work already shipped will do more than chasing 1000 spam directories. Priority order: disavow our toxic links, claim Tier 1 niche/local profiles, add Tier 2 citations, ignore the rest.
