# Easy backlink / DR / authority methods from @hridoyreh

Compiled from Hridoy Reh's X posts (Aug-Sep 2026, his recurring "10 places to get backlinks v1-v9" series + standalone "X DR backlink from [site]" posts). DR = Ahrefs Domain Rating of the source site.

## Important reality check first (read before chasing these)
- **DR is an Ahrefs metric, not a Google ranking factor.** A DR 94 profile link does not guarantee a ranking lift. Most of these give you a *diverse profile + referral traffic + a real dofollow link*, which helps, but none are magic.
- **Many profile/description links are nofollow.** Imgur, SoundCloud, 500px, etc. often mark outbound links nofollow. Still useful for referral traffic and profile diversity, but weight them accordingly.
- **The genuinely high-value ones** (in order): brand-mention reclamation, free-tool linkable assets, infographics, and *relevant niche* directories. The rest are easy volume, not authority.
- For our case (tryagentikai, SG B2B AI agency): the tool-launch directories fit because we have real products (AI receptionist, the cost calculator), and the free-tool + infographic plays we are already doing. Combine these with the real SG sites from the DataForSEO gap (see CURATED-targets.md).

## Type 1 - Content-platform profile / upload links
Create a profile, upload a genuine asset (image, PDF, audio, article), link your site in the bio/description. Easy, high-DR, often nofollow.

| Site | DR | What to upload |
|---|---|---|
| Behance | 94 | Design portfolios, case studies, project pages; link from profile/projects |
| SoundCloud | 94 | Audio, podcasts; website in profile + track descriptions |
| Issuu | 93 | Ebooks, guides, magazines, reports (turn PDFs into flipbooks) |
| Fandom | 92 | Relevant wiki references, added naturally per community rules |
| Mixcloud | 91 | Podcasts, interviews, audio shows; site info in descriptions |
| Imgur | 91 | Images / infographics; keyword-rich title + URL in description |
| 500px | 90 | High-quality photography / graphics; link profile to site |
| HackerNoon-style publishing platform | ~88 | Write a 500+ word technical article, 1 link to your site + internal/external links, "Submit Story" |
| Flickr | high | Infographics / images + URL in description |

## Type 2 - Product / tool launch directories
Submit your SaaS / AI tool / product. Usually a genuine dofollow listing + referral traffic. These fit us (AI receptionist, calculator, the agency itself).

| Directory | DR | Traffic noted |
|---|---|---|
| Open Tools (opentools) | 69 | Submit SaaS / AI tool / product |
| Scroll Launch | 68 | "New Launch" - product details + URL | 64k/mo |
| DevHunt | 63 | "Submit your Dev Tool" - name, description, URL |
| IndieHunt | 62 | List startup / SaaS / indie project |
| OpenAlternative | 51 | "Submit" to list your tool | 180k/mo |
| Powerusers | 33 | AI tools directory |
| SEOFAI | 19 | Submit SEO / AI / marketing tool (low DR but relevant) |

(There are more of these in his older v1-v8 threads: Product Hunt, BetaList, SaaSHub, AlternativeTo, There's An AI For That, Futurepedia, etc. are the usual suspects.)

## Type 3 - Create linkable assets (the actually-durable plays)
- **Infographics.** Make an infographic for your content, publish on Flickr/Imgur, add your URL in the description. Others reuse the image and credit your site with a link. He reports 7 free links this way. (We already produce infographics for every blog post - could syndicate them.)
- **Free tools.** Build a free tool related to your service - ROI calculator, lead-loss calculator, invoice generator, SEO analyzer. Free tools attract links and leads naturally. **We already did this** with the Cost of Lost Leads calculator; that is exactly this play.

## Type 4 - Brand-mention reclamation (highest ROI, $0)
The strongest method he shows. Find places that mention your brand but do not link to you, then ask them to add the link.
1. Use a backlink tool's "top linked pages" / brand-mentions export (Ahrefs, or free: Google alerts / `"your brand" -site:yourdomain`).
2. Filter to unlinked mentions.
3. Email them a short request to add the link.
He reports 24+ quality backlinks for $0 this way. (Caveat for us: tryagentikai is new with almost no mentions yet, so this pays off later as PR builds.)

## Type 5 - His paid product (not a method, just context)
He funnels most threads to "SEO Wins" - a paid database of 150+ SEO/AI SEO hacks (seowins.io). Titles teased include "Get Backlink Directly From Google Official Website", "Earn Free Backlinks From OpenAI/ChatGPT", "Get Brand Mentions Every Time From AI Platforms". Treat these as marketing hooks; the underlying ideas are the profile/directory/reclamation plays above.

## What I would actually do for tryagentikai (priority)
1. **Free-tool + infographic assets** (Type 3) - we already build these; syndicate the infographics to Imgur/Flickr with our URL, and promote the calculator.
2. **Relevant tool/AI directories** (Type 2) - list the agency + tools on OpenAlternative, DevHunt, Open Tools, There's An AI For That, Product Hunt. Genuinely relevant, dofollow.
3. **Brand-mention reclamation** (Type 4) - set up Google Alerts for "Agentic AI Labs" / "tryagentikai" now, reclaim as mentions appear.
4. **A few high-DR profile links** (Type 1) - Behance/Issuu for our infographics and guides, low effort.
5. Skip the low-relevance volume directories; combine the above with the real SG sites in CURATED-targets.md and the disavow of our toxic existing links.
