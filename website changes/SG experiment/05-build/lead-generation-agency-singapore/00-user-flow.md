**Page:** `/lead-generation-agency-singapore/` · **Stage:** 1 (Get Found & Capture) · **Role:** pillar hub + BoFu service page.
**This doc = the FLOW only.** What each section does, in what order, and why. No final copy yet. Approve this, then I write `01-copy.md`.

## 1. Who lands here and what they want

**The visitor:** a Singapore business owner or founder (B2B services, agency, clinic, SaaS, trades) who typed "singapore lead generation" or "lead generation agency." They are BoFu: not learning what lead gen is, they want someone to get them more qualified, booked meetings.

**What they believe walking in:** "I need more leads." Or "I've tried a lead-gen agency / bought lists / hired telemarketers and got ghosted."

**What we want them to believe leaving:** the problem was never the volume of leads, it's the system that catches and converts them, and we build that system inside their tools and stay on the hook for booked calls.

**One primary action:** book the free audit ("show me where I'm losing leads"). CTA points to `/ai-clarity-workshop/` now, swaps to the free tool when it ships.

## 2. Section-by-section flow

### S1 — Hero
- **Job:** name the visitor and the promise in their words; carry the primary keyword naturally.
- **H1 carries:** "Lead Generation Agency Singapore" (as a natural phrase, not stuffed).
- **Angle:** you don't need more leads, you need the system that turns the ones you get into booked calls. Built inside your tools. We own the number.
- **Sub:** one line on the FDE mechanism (a team that builds and runs your lead-gen system, paid on pipeline, not a list you chase yourself).
- **Primary CTA:** Show me where I'm losing leads → audit. **Secondary:** How it works → scroll.
- Keeps "AI" out of the H1 (per home rules); category terms sit in meta + lower body.

### S2 — The uncomfortable truth (the problem, SG-specific)
- **Job:** agree with their pain in their exact words so they feel understood, then reframe.
- **Beats:** you're not short on leads, you're short on follow-up and speed. A lead comes in, nobody calls fast enough, they book the competitor who picked up first. You bought lists and telemarketers and got ghosted. The CRM just sits there.
- Reframes: more leads into a leaky system just costs more. Fix the system first.

### S3 — What we actually do (the forward-deployed difference)
- **Job:** the core differentiator vs every other SG lead-gen agency and vs a self-serve tool.
- **Beats:** we don't hand you a list and disappear. We build the lead-gen system inside your CRM, phone, and inbox: find and qualify the right leads, call them the second they land, follow up like your best salesperson, book the call. We run it, watch the numbers, keep tuning. Paid on pipeline, on the hook for booked calls.
- One line each: found → qualified → booked (ties to the lead journey; this is the Stage 1 hub).

### S4 — The lead journey (mini, hub down-links)
- **Job:** show the 4-stage system and place this page as Stage 1, link down/across.
- Cold lead in (Stage 1, here) → qualify (Stage 2) → warm up & follow up (Stage 3) → book & convert (Stage 4).
- Light visual, links to the other pillars when live (else internal-link TODO, no broken links).

### S5 — What you can start with (the capture systems)
- **Job:** prove we're systematic, in their outcome words. These are the Stage 1 "get found & capture" systems.
- Cards (reuse home top-5 framing, outcomes not features):
  - Find and qualify leads for you (match your best customers, score them, only call the ones worth calling)
  - Speed to lead (call or text the second a lead lands, before they book anyone else)
  - Cold email that gets replies
  - DMs that turn into booked calls (LinkedIn / Instagram)
  - Missed call text back (a missed call becomes a booked appointment)
- Each links to its deep page where one exists.

### S6 — Why us, not a traditional agency or a tool (comparison)
- **Job:** the buyer is comparing us to callbox-style SG lead-gen agencies and to products like PipelineAI Scout. Draw the line.
- Three columns or a compare table (honest, no naming competitors):
  - **A list vendor / telemarketer:** hands you contacts, you chase them, no accountability for booked calls.
  - **A self-serve tool:** you build and babysit it yourself.
  - **Us (forward-deployed):** we build the system inside your tools, run it, and are accountable for booked calls. Singapore hours, your CRM, your numbers.

### S7 — Proof strip (honest only)
- **Job:** credibility without fabrication.
- Built on the tools you already use (GoHighLevel, n8n, Vapi, Retell, HubSpot, Make, Zapier logos, already in repo).
- The systems we build are already running in thousands of businesses.
- Dogfood: ask ChatGPT or Perplexity about getting more customers, our name comes up.
- **No client logos, names, or quotes** until real and permissioned. Testimonial slot stays empty.

### S8 — Who this is for (qualify)
- **Job:** let the wrong-fit self-select out, right-fit lean in.
- For: SG B2B and services that already get enquiries but lose them to slow or missing follow-up; owners tired of buying tools and lists.
- Not for: someone who wants a one-off list and no system.

### S9 — Explore the system (hub down-links, SEO internal linking)
- **Job:** pass authority to the sub-pages and cover the cluster.
- Links: `/b2b-lead-generation-singapore/`, `/seo-agency-singapore/`, `/sem-agency-singapore/`, `/content-marketing-agency-singapore/`, `/social-media-agency-singapore/`, `/ai-visibility-checker/`.
- Any page not live yet → leave a TODO comment, no broken link.

### S10 — FAQ (feeds FAQPage schema, absorbs long-tail)
- **Job:** answer the buyer's real objections; capture question-shaped searches (telemarketing, appointment setting, pricing model, CRM).
- Candidate questions:
  - How is this different from a normal lead generation agency in Singapore?
  - Do you replace my telemarketers / cold callers?
  - What CRM do you work with? (we build inside the tools you already use)
  - How fast do you contact a new lead? (speed to lead)
  - Is this just a chatbot? (no, it's a system your team runs on)
  - What does it cost / how is pricing structured? (paid on pipeline framing; hold exact SGD until pricing bands are set)
  - Do you work with Singapore businesses / in SG hours?

### S11 — Final CTA
- **Job:** one clear next step.
- Headline: find out where you're losing leads. Sub: a short look at how leads move through your business, what winning them back is worth, before you commit.
- CTA: Show me where I'm losing leads → audit (tool when live).

## 3. SEO + schema (for the build stage, not copy)
- **Title/H1/slug** carry "Lead Generation Agency Singapore." Secondary terms (singapore lead generation, leads generation singapore, b2b lead generation singapore) sit in S1 sub, S8, S10 naturally.
- **Schema:** Service + FAQPage (from S10) + BreadcrumbList.

## 4. Hard rules carried from home-page-copy.md
- Buyer lexicon only. Say: leads, customers, follow-up, booked, chasing, ghosted, no-show, speed to lead, missed call text back. Avoid: gap, revenue leak, orchestrate, workflow, deploy, "your stack," SDR/ICP/quota jargon in body.
- "AI" and "automation" stay low, never in the H1.
- No em or en dashes anywhere. No invented stats, clients, or testimonials.
