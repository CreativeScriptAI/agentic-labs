# Stage 0 — User Flow: Answer Engine Optimization pillar

**Status:** awaiting Aditya approval. This is the macro flow (storyboard), NOT copy. Copy comes in Stage 1.

**Page:** `/answer-engine-optimization/` · **Type:** SEO pillar (Get Found cluster) + funnel Stage 1.
**Target keywords:** answer engine optimization (2,400), GEO / generative engine optimization (4,400), ai search optimization (1,300), how to rank in ChatGPT (KD 1), ai seo. Secondary: "how to show up in AI answers", "get cited by ChatGPT", "llms.txt".

**The core insight the page sells:** search moved from ten blue links to one AI answer with a few names in it. AEO (also called GEO / AI SEO) is the work of getting your business into that answer. This page is the authoritative explainer AND the doorway to our tool + our done-for-you fix.

**Dogfood rule:** an AEO page must itself be AEO-optimized. Answer-first structure, crisp definitions, clean headings, FAQ schema, so LLMs cite THIS page. It is the proof of the method.

---

## Who lands here + the two paths
- **The searcher (TOFU, learning):** typed "what is answer engine optimization" / "how to rank in ChatGPT". Wants a clear answer. Path: hook → definition → mechanism → checklist → measure it (tool) → maybe book.
- **The buyer (MOFU, shopping):** knows they are invisible in AI, wants it fixed. Path: hook → "we do it for you" → proof → book.

Both funnel to: run the AI Visibility Checker, and/or book the fix.

## Section-by-section flow (top to bottom)

**1. Hero**
- Job: name the shift and the term in one breath. "AEO is how you get your business named when buyers ask AI."
- User sees: the eyebrow term (Answer Engine Optimization), a plain-language one-liner, and two doors: primary "Check if AI names you" (to the tool), secondary "Get it done for you" (workshop).
- Carries the head term in H1.

**2. Definition block (answer-first, built for AI citation)**
- Job: the crisp, quotable definition an LLM will lift. What AEO is, that it is also called GEO and AI SEO, one sentence each.
- Format: short, declarative, skimmable. This block is engineered to be the cited answer.

**3. Why it matters now (the shift)**
- Job: stakes. Ten links then, one answer now, three names in it. If you are not named, you are not in the running.
- Reuse the old-vs-new-search idea (visual), lighter than on the tool page.

**4. AEO vs SEO vs GEO (clear the confusion)**
- Job: capture the comparison intent and resolve terminology. Same goal (get found), new surface (AI answers). A compact comparison, not a fight.

**5. How AI answer engines decide who to name (the mechanism)**
- Job: the actual "how". What ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI, Gemini, Grok, Claude use to pick names: can they read your site, do they trust it, does your content answer the question, are you cited elsewhere.
- This is the meat that earns the ranking + citations.

**6. The AEO checklist (what to actually do)**
- Job: the practical, do-this list, mapped 1:1 to what the tool checks: llms.txt, structured data, a clean text/markdown version, crawlability by AI bots, content depth that answers buyer questions, third-party citations/authority.
- Each item: what it is, why the AI needs it, red/green. Doubles as the bridge to the tool.

**7. How to rank in ChatGPT (and each engine)**
- Job: capture the high-intent long-tail ("how to rank in ChatGPT", KD 1) and seed the blog spokes. Short, per-engine practical notes.
- Links down to future blog spokes.

**8. Measure it → the AI Visibility Checker (tool bridge)**
- Job: turn reading into action. "Do not guess. See where you stand." Embed/CTA to `/ai-visibility-checker/`.
- The money bridge: pillar reader becomes tool user becomes lead.

**9. The fix / we do it for you (primary CTA, differentiator)**
- Job: bridge from "here is how AEO works" to "we are the team that does it." GTM engineering team: the technical work, the content, the proof over time. You do not learn AEO, we make you show up.
- CTA: book the clarity workshop.

**10. Proof (dogfood, honest)**
- Job: credibility. We did this to our own site; ask ChatGPT about getting more customers with AI and we come up. No fabricated numbers.

**11. FAQ (objections + long-tail + FAQPage schema)**
- Job: answer "what is AEO / GEO / AI SEO", "is AEO the same as SEO", "how long does it take", "can I do it myself", "how do I get into ChatGPT". Targets long-tail, powers schema, feeds AI answers.

**12. Final CTA + internal links**
- Job: last conversion (check visibility / book the fix) and wire the cluster.
- Links: to `/ai-visibility-checker/`; up to the Stage 1 capture pillar `/lead-generation-agency-singapore/` (built in parallel); to blog spokes ("how to rank in ChatGPT", "what is llms.txt") as they exist; from the tool page, homepage Resources, footer, llms.txt.
