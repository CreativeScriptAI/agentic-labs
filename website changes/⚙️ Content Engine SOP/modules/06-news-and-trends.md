# Module 06 — News & Trends

Governed by `../00-ORCHESTRATOR.md`.

For timely content (latest tech, trends, "what changed in 2026"):

- **Google Trends** (free): rising queries, breakout terms, geo interest. Cross-check keyword direction.
- **Free sources:** WebSearch / WebFetch, Reddit (audience pain + real phrasing), X / LinkedIn (via the Chrome extension), Hacker News, vendor changelogs, Perplexity.
- **Rule:** a trend only becomes content if it ties to the reader's problem and one of our journey stages. Trend-for-the-sake-of-it is a reporter piece; kill it or tie it to the fight (Reporter to Patient, Module 01).
