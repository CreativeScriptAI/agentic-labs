# Module 01 — Writing

Read before writing any words. Governed by `../00-ORCHESTRATOR.md`.

## 1. Pick the voice (two surfaces, two voices)

- **WEBSITE / PILLAR-PAGE VOICE (default).** The business buyer's own words: leads, customers, follow-up, booked, chasing, no-shows, the CRM that just sits there. Problem-first, plain, concrete, calm-confident. "AI" stays low, never in a headline. Reference: `SG experiment/04-copy/home-page-copy.md` and that project's `_COPY_LEXICON_ALL_SUBS.md`. **Use for all pillar and service pages.**
- **PERSONAL / SOCIAL VOICE (founder brand only).** Aditya yapping to a friend at 1am, raw, vulnerable, lowercase, rupees, India 18 to 24. Reference: `content_structure_rules.md`. **Never for B2B pages.**

Decide by surface: a page that sells a service = website voice. A post in Aditya's own voice = personal voice.

## 2. Universal rules (both voices)

- **3-Factor Validator (final gate):** entertaining + valuable + emotional. Name each, pass/fail, fix fails.
- **Problem-first (Reporter to Patient):** open on the reader's problem, not on us or a feature. We are evidence inside their problem, not the subject.
- **Plain words, lived specifics.** Simple vocabulary, real scenes over grand claims. If a line could sit in any generic LinkedIn post, cut it.
- **Three Rules filter** (headlines/CTAs): can I visualize it, can I falsify it, can no competitor say it. Three yeses or rewrite.

## 3. Kill AI-writing tells on sight

No puffery ("testament to", "vital role"), no "-ing" tails ("highlighting the shift"), no negative parallelism ("not just X, it's Y"), no rule-of-three synonyms, no "it's worth noting", no vague authority ("studies show"), no welded transitions (moreover, furthermore, in conclusion), no title-case headings, no emoji in prose, no em dashes. Full list: `content_structure_rules.md` + `Signs-of-AI-Writing-Reference.md`.
